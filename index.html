          Help and shortcuts

          • Tap an ayah to recite it, highlight it, bookmark it, read its translation or share a link. Shift-click a second ayah to select a range.

          • Mark mistakes while your student recites: tap the word. It goes into their practice list in the notebook.

          • Hide text to test memory. Tap a word to reveal it, or press N for the next word. Press T again to show only the first word of each ayah.

          • Highlighter ink sits under the letters so the text stays sharp. Hold Shift for a straight line.

          • Every student gets their own drawings, highlights, bookmarks and notes. Switch from the name at the top.

          • Tajweed colours give each rule its own colour. The key is on the Tajweed tab of the notebook, where you can hide rules to practise one at a time.

          • Bookmarks save a page for the open student. Tap one in the list to go straight to it.

          • Your mushaf: add the PDF of a printed mushaf in Settings to teach from its own pages. Press I to switch between the Uthmani, IndoPak and your mushaf.

          Halaqa

          A Quran classroom for teachers. Open any of the 604 pages of the Madani mushaf, point, draw and highlight while you teach, and keep a notebook for every student.

          Sources

          • Quran text: Tanzil Project (Uthmani script), shown unchanged.
          • Text, translations and search: AlQuran Cloud API.
          • Recitations: Islamic Network CDN.
          • Tajweed marking: AlQuran Cloud tajweed edition.
          • IndoPak text: the IndoPak edition of the open Quran API by fawazahmed0.
          • Fonts for IndoPak and Urdu: Lateef and Scheherazade New by SIL, Noto Nastaliq Urdu by Google (SIL Open Font License).
          • Your mushaf: the pages come from your own PDF and stay on your device. The page index for the 13-line colour-coded tajweed mushaf was made by Halaqa.
          • Fonts: Amiri and Amiri Quran by Khaled Hosny, Instrument Sans (SIL Open Font License).
